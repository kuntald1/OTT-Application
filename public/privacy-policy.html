Privacy Policy

theomy — last updated: September 2026

theomy ("we", "our", "us") operates the theomy mobile app and website (together, the "Service"), a platform for streaming Bengali theatre productions and related content. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use theomy:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and password (or social login identifiers) when you register or sign in.
  • Usage data: videos you watch, watch progress and history, your "My List", likes, and content you interact with, so we can show you continue-watching and recommendations.
  • Community activity: blog comments, community room posts, and likes you post within the app.
  • Payment information: subscription and ticket purchases are processed by our payment partner, Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details ourselves — Razorpay handles and secures that information under its own privacy policy.
  • Device and technical data: device type, operating system, app version, and crash/diagnostic logs, used to keep the app working correctly.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in
  • To stream video content to you and remember your watch progress
  • To process subscription payments and event ticket purchases
  • To show relevant recommendations and continue-watching rows
  • To operate community features (blog comments, community rooms)
  • To display advertising within free/ad-supported video content
  • To respond to support requests and communicate service updates
  • To detect, prevent, and fix technical issues

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Razorpay — to process payments for subscriptions and tickets.
  • Bunny.net — our video hosting/CDN provider, used to deliver video streams.
  • Google (IMA SDK) — to serve advertising within video content on the free tier.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, only where required by law.

4. Social Sharing Features

If you choose to share content from theomy to Facebook or Instagram Stories, the shared image/link is sent directly to that platform through its own app — this is governed by Facebook's and Instagram's own privacy policies, not this one.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and usage data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see below).

6. Your Choices

  • You can edit your profile information from within the app.
  • You can request a copy of your data, or request that we delete your account and data.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

theomy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
