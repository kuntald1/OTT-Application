Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: October 1, 2026  ·  Rednose Mediatech Private Limited (Brand: www.theomy.com)

Welcome to Rednose Mediatech Private Limited (Brand Name: www.theomy.com) ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our theatre lovers, subscribers, and creators. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, mobile apps, or connected TV apps (collectively, the "Platform") to stream Theomy plays, live theatrical broadcasts, monologues, and performing arts content.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Credentials: Name, email address, password, phone number.
  • Profile & Preferences: Favorite genres (tragedy, musical, avant-garde, classical), watchlists, favorite playwrights or acting troupes.
  • Ticketing & Transaction Data: Billing address, payment card details (processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant third-party gateways like Stripe/Razorpay), and virtual ticket/pass purchase history for live-streamed theatrical events.
  • Interactive Data: Live chat messages during simultaneous Theomy streaming events, Q&A submissions, or community forum posts.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Technical & Device Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system, app version, and screen resolution.
  • Streaming Telemetry: Playback buffer events, watch duration, completion rates, and timestamps for specific scenes or acts (used to improve streaming quality and analyze engagement for theatrical licensing partners).
  • Cookies & Tracking: Essential session cookies, analytics cookies, and DRM handshake tokens.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Deliver Theatrical Content: Stream high-definition video/audio of stage productions, manage live-stream access keys, and sync multi-camera feeds if applicable.
  • Honor Licensing Agreements: Aggregate anonymous viewership metrics required by playwright licensing agencies (e.g., Music Theatre International, Concord Theatricals) or independent theatre troupes.
  • Personalize Discovery: Recommend plays, backstage interviews, or regional repertory theatre showcases based on your viewing history.
  • Enforce Digital Rights Management (DRM): Detect unauthorized screen-capturing, session-hijacking, or concurrent stream limit breaches.
  • Communication: Send transactional receipts, cast-update alerts, premiere notifications for digital opening nights, and customer support replies.

3. How We Share Your Information

We never sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting (AWS/GCP), CDN providers, payment gateways, and customer support ticketing tools.
  • Theatrical Rights & Production Partners: Aggregated, anonymized demographic and viewership count data provided to partner theatre companies or independent producers for royalty/performance reporting.
  • Legal & Safety Obligations: Compliance with subpoenas, copyright infringement notices (DMCA/local equivalents), or protection of intellectual property rights for staged works.

4. Content Protection and Digital Rights Management (DRM)

Theatrical productions stream under strict intellectual property licenses. To protect playwrights, actors, and creators:

  • We deploy browser- and device-level DRM encryption (Widevine, FairPlay, PlayReady).
  • We monitor suspicious background processes, video-hooking software, or abnormal multi-IP login velocity.
  • Attempting to bypass DRM to record or redistribute live stage performances will result in immediate account termination and potential legal recourse by rights holders.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • Financial transaction records are retained for 7 years (or local tax requirement duration).
  • Anonymized streaming telemetry may be retained long-term for artistic trend analysis and historical platform metrics.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, DPDP Act, etc.), you have the right to:

  • Access/Export: Request a copy of your personal data and viewing history archive.
  • Rectify: Update inaccurate account or billing details.
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account (note: active season passes or legal obligations may delay full deletion of financial ledgers).
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing newsletters or manage cookie preferences via our Cookie Consent Manager.

To exercise these rights, email us at: upamanyu@theomy.com

7. Children's Privacy

Our platform features content rated for various maturity levels (from family-friendly puppet/youth theatre to mature dramatic themes). Accounts for users under 18 must be created and managed by a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in digital streaming tech or theatrical licensing scope. Material changes will be highlighted via email or platform banner 14 days prior to taking effect.
