Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: 1st October, 2026  ·  Last Updated: 1st October, 2026  ·  Rednose Mediatech Private Limited (Brand: www.theomy.com)

At Rednose Mediatech Private Limited (www.theomy.com), we specialize in streaming performing arts, stage plays, experimental drama, and digital theatre productions. Because digital media delivery differs from physical goods—and live/recorded performing arts licensing involves strict creator rights—this Refund and Cancellation Policy outlines your rights and our obligations.

1. Overview

This policy applies to all subscription, Pay-Per-View, and virtual ticket purchases made on the Platform.

2. Subscription Cancellations

  • Monthly / Annual Plans: You may cancel your subscription at any time via your account settings (Account > Billing > Cancel Subscription).
  • Access Period: Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle. You will retain full access to our theatre catalog until that period expires.
  • No Pro-Rata Refunds: We do not provide pro-rated refunds for unused days or weeks remaining in an active billing period, except where mandated by local consumer protection law.

3. Pay-Per-View (PPV) / Digital Ticket Purchases

Some exclusive stage premiers, live-streamed theatrical performances, or festival repertory passes are sold via Pay-Per-View (PPV):

  • Before Event/Access: PPV bookings for scheduled live-stream plays can be cancelled for a full refund up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled stream/premiere time.
  • After Stream Start / VOD Unlock: Once a live stream has commenced or a digital show pass has been unlocked for On-Demand (VOD) viewing, no refunds or cancellations will be issued, as digital delivery rights and artist royalties are locked.

4. Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds are strictly limited to the following technical or administrative scenarios:

  • Duplicate Billing: Accidental double-charging for the same billing cycle (verified via transaction logs).
  • Unresolved Technical Failure: If you are unable to stream any content due to a platform-wide outage lasting more than 48 consecutive hours, or where our support team verifies a persistent server-side playback failure on our end that we cannot resolve within 3 business days of a ticket submission.
Note: Poor local internet connectivity, incompatible legacy hardware, or personal dislike of an artistic performance/play do not qualify for a refund.

5. Refund Request Process

  • Step 1: Email support at upamanyu@theomy.com or use the In-App Help / Live Chat.
  • Step 2: Include your Registered Email, Transaction/Order ID, and a brief description (with screenshots/screen recordings if reporting a playback bug).
  • Step 3: Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days (subject to bank/payment gateway processing times).

6. Creator/Artist Rights Disclaimer

Portions of revenue generated from specific drama/theatre rentals go directly to independent playwrights, theater troupes, and copyright holders. Consequently, special virtual-ticket sales tied to charitable theater fundraisers or limited-run festival screenings are final sale and clearly marked as non-refundable at checkout.
